# AI_USE — Super Intelligence (SI) disclosure

Space Apps 2026 Project Submission Guide: "Code and data: Must acknowledge SI
generation in descriptive text and metadata." This file is that text.

- **Idea and design:** the team's (CrewWatch concept, three-detector honest
  comparison, reuse of the internship flight code). Written in the team's internal plan
  by the team lead before AI coding started.
- **Code generated with AI assistance (Claude Code, Anthropic):** everything
  listed under "Built during the hackathon" in `PRIOR_WORK.md`
  (`features/crewwatch_hold.*`, `features/bridge.py`, `sim/`, `model/`,
  `eval/`, `explain/`, `web/`). The team reviews, runs and owns it.
- **Not AI-generated in this hackathon:** `vendor/` (tinyinfer, cfs-m2s150 —
  written by Orhun during the internship).
- **Text:** the submission text (`docs/PROJECT_PAGE.md`), the video captions
  (`docs/VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`, built into `web/index.html?demo`) and the slide
  outline were written with AI assistance at the team's request and
  disclosed in the "Use of SI" field of the project page.
- **Images/video:** none generated by AI. `docs/crewwatch-demo.mp4` is a
  screen recording of our own panel (`web/index.html?demo`), captured
  automatically; the captions in it are AI-written text, disclosed here.
- **Data:** no AI-generated data. Normal data is real (PPG-DaLiA); faults are
  produced by deterministic code (`sim/faults.py`) whose parameters are listed
  in `data/SOURCES.md`.

Metadata: generated source files carry the line
"Written with AI assistance (Claude Code), see AI_USE.md" in their header or
are listed above.
